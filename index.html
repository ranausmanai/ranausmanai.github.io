About

I work in the space between research and production. The through-line across my projects is simple: build AI systems that are useful, inspectable, and honest about their limits.

That includes verifier-driven inference, agent workflows, interpretability experiments, behavioral evaluation, and constrained training studies on consumer or commodity hardware. I am not an academic in the formal sense, but I do publish technical work when the result is solid enough to stand on its own.

Topics I care about most:

  • LLM evaluation and reliability
  • Agent workflows and tool use
  • Small-model and local inference research
  • Behavioral analysis and interpretability
  • Practical AI systems under real operating constraints
