About

Practical AI work, not academic theater.

I am an Engineering Manager, Applied AI and LLM Systems leader with experience across Groupon, Toptal, Jazz, consulting, and earlier data science work. My core interest is building AI systems that survive contact with the real world: cost constraints, noisy feedback, production quality, and changing requirements.

Outside work, I publish experiments on small open models, verifier-driven inference, agent workflows, and behavioral evaluation. The goal is not to perform “research aesthetics.” It is to learn something defensible, build something usable, and make the tradeoffs legible.

That is why most of the work here lives in the space between product and research: efficient model training, local inference, mechanistic interpretability, reliability loops, and open evaluation tooling.

LLM systems and evaluation Verifier-driven inference, reliability pipelines, behavioral analysis, and model quality under operational constraints.
Agent workflows Tool use, orchestration, coding agents, and developer-facing systems that turn models into useful workflows.
Efficient experimentation Consumer-hardware research, small-model training, and disciplined iteration instead of expensive brute force.
